# VEDRISHI AI - PROJECT VISION

## MISSION
Duniya bhar ke Hindu users ko ek AI-powered spiritual guide dena jo:
- Shrimad Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, Ramayana ke basis par guidance de
- Compassionate aur non-judgmental tone mein baat kare
- Free mein basic services de, premium ke liye nominal charge kare
- Koi bhi andhavishwas ya fraud na failaye

## TARGET USERS
1. **Primary**: 18-35 saal ke Hindu youth (global)
   - Career stress, breakups, mental health issues
   - Social stigma ki wajah se kisi se baat nahi kar paate
   - Technology comfortable hain
2. **Secondary**: 35+ age group
   - Spiritual seekers
   - Daily puja/archana wale log
   - Parents jo bacchon ko sanskriti sikhana chahte hain

## CORE FEATURES
1. **Daily Margdarshan**: Subah ek shloka + uska meaning
2. **Sankat Nivaran Chat**: Problem batao → Gita/Ramayan se solution
3. **Virtual Puja**: User ke naam se diya jalana, mantra jaap
4. **Voice Interaction**: AI ki aawaz mein jawab (ElevenLabs)
5. **Kundali Analysis**: Basic Vedic astrology (premium)

## REVENUE MODEL
1. Free: 3 questions/day
2. Premium ₹199-299/month: Unlimited + features
3. Digital Dakshina: ₹11-101 micro-donations
4. E-Commerce: Rudraksha, pooja samagri (affiliate)
5. Kundali Reports: ₹99 per report

## COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE
- Hindi + Sanskrit + English trilingual
- Authentic scripture-based (not random gyan)
- Compassionate tone (not robotic)
- Free tier available
- No fake promises or miracle claims

## LEGAL COMPLIANCE
- IT Act 2000 compliant
- IPC 295A awareness (no religious insult)
- Clear AI disclaimer everywhere
- No medical/legal/financial advice
- Crisis helpline redirect for self-harm

## TIMELINE
- Week 1-2: AI Model Training
- Week 3-4: MVP Build (Web + WhatsApp)
- Week 5-6: Testing + Monetization
- Week 7-8: Launch + Marketing
- Month 3+: Company registration (Vietnam)
